A SATURDAY WITH US

06:45

shiraho port

we meet at the end of the pier. the sea is still flat and the colour of milk. coffee in a thermos.

07:30

the boat leaves

twenty minutes out to the reef. the captain doesn’t talk until he has seen the water.

08:10

you learn eight fish

on the boat, with cards. the butterflyfish, the parrotfish, the one we want to go down. you’ll be surprised how fast you learn.

  1. butterflyfish
  2. parrotfish
  3. humphead wrasse
  4. coral grouper
  5. chromis
  6. giant clam
  7. crown-of-thorns
  8. green turtle

08:40

first dive · the belt

fifty metres of tape at −8 m. you swim on the left, we swim on the right. you count, the pencil writes.

09:50

surface interval

an hour on the boat to let the nitrogen go. tea, rice balls, and we copy the slates into the logbook.

10:40

second dive · the coral

a point every fifty centimetres. coral, white coral, dead coral, sand. you’ll never look at a reef the same way.

12:30

your line

back at the institute your numbers go on the map, and your name next to them. it stays there. next year someone will swim your line again.

WHAT TO BRING

nothing.
almost.

WE BRING

  • the boat, and the captain
  • tanks, weights, a 3 mm wetsuit
  • the slate and the pencil
  • the tape, fifty metres
  • tea, rice balls
  • the eight fish cards

YOU BRING

  • an open water licence (or come snorkel: the moat is two metres deep)
  • a towel
  • reef-safe sunscreen, or none
  • twelve years or more
  • patience with the captain
